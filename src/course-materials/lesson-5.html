Lesson 5: Releasing the Tongue

In the early stages of practice, many people notice sensations of tension or fatigue in the area beneath the tongue. This region contains many muscles that often remain in a state of increased tone in everyday life.

For this reason, gentle relaxation and careful stretching of the tongue muscles are an important part of the practice. This helps reduce excess tension and creates more comfortable conditions for further work.

In this video, you will be guided through a technique focused on relaxing the sublingual area and promoting a greater sense of ease and freedom in the tongue and lower face.

The exercise should be performed gently and without force. It is recommended to hold the position for 30 seconds to 1 minute, always prioritizing comfort.

At the beginning of the course, this practice may be performed daily for the first 10–14 days, and afterward as needed.
